# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the
work. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to Szymon Zywert at the contact address listed on the
repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is available at the link above and
applies in full.
